## Recurrence conclusion

The frozen contract controls the previously proven protocol failures. The remaining Chronicle risk is product truth: the baseline cannot yet show whether a displayed record is canonical LIVE, controlled demo, or historical proof. The remediation therefore targets mode boundaries, canonical read integration, transaction/recovery visibility, evidence provenance, and current-state documentation without changing contract source.
